About Me

Hi, I’m Alex Rivera, a licensed Professional Engineer (P.E.) with over 10 years of experience in civil engineering and infrastructure design. I specialize in residential and commercial site development, stormwater systems, and sustainable urban planning.

I believe that great engineering blends technical precision with creative problem-solving. Every project I take on is designed not just to meet code — but to serve communities for decades to come.

When I’m not on-site or reviewing blueprints, you’ll find me mentoring young engineers, hiking near construction sites for inspiration, or sharing behind-the-scenes project stories on Instagram.
